Add the `stat` dashboard widget (RFC 0001, slice 1): a `measure` of `count`, `sum`, `avg`, `min`, `max` or `countDistinct` over a table view's records, with `product: [a, b]` for sum and avg. `describeContract()` lists the measure ops, the validator checks view, field ids and field types, and `CountWidgetProps` gains an optional preformatted `text`.
